Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Do It keeps everything on your device. We don’t collect anything, we don’t have servers that receive your data, and there are no accounts, ads or tracking.

No accounts No ads No tracking No analytics Stays on your device
The details

Your data stays on your device

Your plans, habits, reminders, countdowns, goals, focus sessions, settings, name and profile picture are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, synced or sent to us or anyone else.

We collect nothing

Do It does not collect, store or share personal information. There is no sign-up, no account and no login. We don’t use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and we don’t sell or share data, because we never have it.

Notifications are local

Reminders and nudges are scheduled on your device by your phone’s own notification system. They are not sent from a server, and turning them off in the app or in your phone’s settings stops them completely.

Photos

If you choose a profile picture, Do It opens your photo library only for the photo you pick. A small copy is kept with your settings on your device. Nothing else in your library is read or uploaded.

Backups are yours

“Back up to a file” creates a file that you save wherever you choose, such as the Files app. We never see it. “Restore from a backup” reads only the file you pick.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Erase everything, or delete the app. Because your data only exists on your device, that removes it completely.

The web version

If you use Do It in a web browser, your data is stored in that browser on your device, in the same way. Our hosting provider delivers the app’s files to your browser and, like any website host, may keep standard technical request logs. We don’t use these to identify you.

Children

Do It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If Do It ever starts collecting data, we’ll say so here and in the app first.
